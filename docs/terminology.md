# Terminology

This glossary defines the terms used throughout the Regulatory Submission
Processing Portal.

> These definitions are fictional demonstration content and do not represent
> the terminology, policies, or processes of any government organization.

## Submission

An application and its supporting information created for regulatory review.
Each Submission has a unique reference number and a Submission Status.

## Applicant

The organization representative who prepares, saves, and submits a Submission.

## Supporting Document

A file selected by an Applicant to provide additional information for a
Submission. The demonstration portal retains file metadata only and does not
store file contents.

## Submission Status

The current stage of a Submission in its lifecycle.

### Draft

The Submission has been saved by the Applicant but has not been submitted.

### Submitted

The Submission has been received by the portal and is awaiting review.

### Under Review

The Submission is being assessed by review staff.

### Additional Information Required

The review is paused until the Applicant provides requested information.

### Approved

The review has been completed with a positive decision.

### Closed

Processing is complete and no further action is expected.
